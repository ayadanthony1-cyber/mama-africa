Our Mission

Mama Africa Impact is a grassroots development organization focused on high-poverty regions of Tanzania. We work alongside local leaders to deliver practical, long-term solutions — not short-term aid.

What We Do

Clean Water Access

Community-managed water systems that reduce disease and save families hours each day.

Education Support

School supplies, teacher support, and infrastructure for rural primary schools.

Community Livelihoods

Agricultural inputs and skills training to increase household income sustainably.

Our Impact Approach

We start small, measure results, and scale what works. Every project includes clear metrics, community ownership, and public reporting.

  • ✔ Local leadership & ownership
  • ✔ Transparent finances
  • ✔ Measurable outcomes
  • ✔ Long-term sustainability
